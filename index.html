HROOT Documentation

Hamburg Registration and Organization Online Tool · WISO Research Lab, University of Hamburg

🚀 Interactive One-Line Installation

HROOT runs on precompiled Docker containers. Execute this one-line command in your Linux, macOS, or WSL terminal to begin interactive installation:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiso-forschungslabor/hroot-documentation/master/install.sh | bash
